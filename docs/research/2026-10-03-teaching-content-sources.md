## Corrections to earlier logs

- The 2026-09-26 log assumed ILO booklets dated after 3 May 2023 are CC BY 4.0. The 2023 Côte d'Ivoire booklet carries standard ILO copyright. Lessons must be written in our own words with citation, or permission requested.
- The booklet catalogued as "West Africa" is Africa-wide and contains nothing West African.

## Errors found inside the sources (recompute, never copy figures)

ILO-CI: quiz F3 key contradicts its chapter; Koffi's day-labour cost is 150 000 in the text and 720 000 in the table; a seeds line is 58 000 on the card and 158 000 in the answer; loan totals are off by centimes; AVEC is classed semi-formal on one page and formal on another. ILO-AF and ILO-GH have similar slips (salary 600 vs 500; a savings plan showing 100 where the figures give 211.11; missing answer keys).

## Still not found

- A beginner-level source on **what a portfolio is and on rebalancing** (only a paraphrase of a FINRA page).
- **Investor education from the regional market itself**: BRVM training is video only; the AMF-UMOA site returned no readable page.
- Investment **fees**: one source (SEC); no broker or fund in Burkina publishes a readable fee grid.
- A source defining **inflation** for beginners; general **taxes**; **pensions** (the CNSS site was defaced on 2026-10-03); **entrepreneurship** beyond separating business and household budgets.
- **Questions from real West African beginners**: the closest material is the ILO quizzes (publisher-written), the FinScope 2016 finding that young people in Burkina most want to know "how to acquire a loan and how to invest and save", and broker FAQs.
- The BCEAO regional programme's modules (PREF-UEMOA).
